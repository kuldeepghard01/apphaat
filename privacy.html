Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

AppHaat (“we”) runs the AppHaat marketplace for apps and websites. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact details are on the Contact page.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: your email, your password (stored in a protected form, never in plain text), and your name or profile picture if you sign in with Google, GitHub or another provider.
  • Profile: the display name and “about” text you choose to add.
  • Listings: the name, link, price, revenue, profit, traffic, description, images and other details you enter. These are shown publicly when the listing is live.
  • Messages and offers: chats, offers and requests you send to other users.
  • Activity: listings you save, views of listings (counted without showing who viewed), notifications, and your notification settings.
  • Payments: the amount, date and payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner and are not stored by us.
  • Device information: if you turn on push alerts, a device address that lets us send them. We also keep simple technical data such as your browser type for security.

3. How we use it

To run your account and the marketplace, show listings, connect buyers and sellers, verify ownership, process payments, send notifications you ask for, prevent fraud and abuse, improve the service, and follow the law.

4. Who can see what

Live listings are public. Other users see your display name and profile text if you add them, and the messages and offers you send to them. We do not show your email address to other users. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Services we use

We use trusted providers to run AppHaat: Supabase (database, login and file storage), GitHub Pages (hosting), Razorpay (payments), sign-in providers you choose (such as Google or GitHub), and browser push services if you turn on alerts. To verify ownership, our server may open the link in your listing and look up public DNS records. These providers handle data under their own policies, and some may store data outside India.

5a. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember choices such as your currency and last login method. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is active and as long as needed for legal, payment and fraud-prevention reasons. You can delete your listings yourself. To ask us to delete your account or data, write to .

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, turn off push alerts and view notifications in the app, and stop using the service at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

9. Children

AppHaat is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
