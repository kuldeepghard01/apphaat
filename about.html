About AppHaat

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AppHaat is an online marketplace where people buy and sell apps and websites. Sellers list what they own, buyers find it, and the two talk to each other directly on the platform.

What we do

  • List and discover: sellers add their Android app, iOS app or website with its price and numbers. Buyers search by type, category, price, revenue and traffic.
  • Verified ownership: sellers can prove they own a listing with a small code. Verified listings get a badge.
  • Offers and chat: buyers can send an offer, and sellers can accept, decline or counter it. Everyone can chat inside AppHaat.
  • Buyer requests: buyers can post what they want to buy, and sellers with a matching listing can contact them.
  • Featured spots: sellers can pay to place a listing at the top of the home page for a week.
  • Value estimate: a free tool that gives a rough value range for a website or app.

What we do not do

We do not take a commission on sales, and we do not hold or move the money for a deal between a buyer and a seller. Deals are made between the two people. See our Terms of Service.

How we earn

We charge sellers a one-time account fee and an optional fee for featured spots. See Pricing.
