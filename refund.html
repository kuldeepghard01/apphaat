Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AppHaat charges only for two things: a one-time account activation fee for sellers, and an optional fee for featured spots. We do not take a commission on sales, and all payments to AppHaat are one-time (nothing renews automatically).

1. Account activation fee

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying, as long as you have not yet submitted a listing. After you submit a listing, the fee is not refundable, because the account has been used.

2. Featured spots

If your spot has not started, you can cancel and get a full refund. Once a spot has started, it is not refundable. If a spot did not appear as promised because of a fault on AppHaat, we will extend it for the lost time or refund the unused part.

3. Failed or double payments

If money was taken but your account or spot was not activated, or you were charged twice, write to us with the payment reference. We will fix it or refund the extra amount.

4. When we do not refund

We do not refund fees if an account or listing is removed for breaking our Terms of Service, for example fake or illegal listings.

5. How to ask

Email with the email on your account and your payment reference. We reply within 2 working days.

6. How long a refund takes

Once approved, refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.

7. Cancelling

You can stop using AppHaat at any time. Because payments are one-time, there is nothing to cancel.
