Pricing

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Buying is free. Sellers pay a small one-time account fee to list, and can optionally pay for a featured spot. There is no commission on sales.

PricesLoading current prices...

Prices are in US dollars. Online payments are charged in Indian rupees at the rate shown above. Prices may change in the future, and the price you see when you pay is the price you pay.

What you get

  • Buyers (free): browse and search all listings, save favourites, send offers, chat with sellers, and post what you want to buy.
  • Seller account fee (one time): lets you list apps and websites, get verified, receive offers and messages, and see your listing analytics. The first sign-ups announced as free do not pay this fee.
  • Featured spot (optional, per 7 days): places your live, verified listing in the featured area at the top of the home page. Spot 1 is shown first.

Payments, taxes and refunds

You can pay by UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through our payment partner. We never see or store your card details. Applicable taxes, if any, are shown before you pay. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy. There are no recurring charges, so nothing renews automatically.
