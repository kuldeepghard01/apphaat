Questions people ask

Is AppHaat free to use?

Browsing, saving, offers and chat are free for buyers. Sellers pay a small one-time account fee (the first sign-ups are free) and can pay for an optional featured spot. We take no commission on sales. See Pricing.

How does verification work?

We give you a small code. You place it on your website or app page, then tap Check now. If we find it, your listing gets the Verified badge. It takes about two minutes.

Does AppHaat handle the payment for a deal?

No. Buyer and seller agree the payment between themselves. For larger amounts, use a trusted escrow service and check ownership before you pay.

What happens when an offer is accepted?

Both sides have agreed on a price. They continue in chat to finish the deal, and the seller marks the listing as sold. Only deals with an accepted offer appear in Recently sold.

Which currencies can I use?

Listings can be priced in US dollars or Indian rupees, and visitors can switch the display currency at any time.

Is every listing checked?

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. The Verified badge shows that ownership was proven. Numbers such as revenue and traffic are entered by sellers, so always do your own checks.

Is my data safe?

We never store card details. Removed listings are kept safely, and backups are taken regularly. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do refunds work?

See our Refund Policy. Fees for featured spots that have not started can be refunded.
